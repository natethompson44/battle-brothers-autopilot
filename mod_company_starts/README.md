# Company Starts

Custom starting origins built for autonomous play. They show up on the new-campaign screen next
to the stock ones. Vanilla only for now.

## Wolfeo's Company (difficulty: easy)

- **Wolfeo the Nightblade**, your avatar. Swordmaster base, level 8, a named greatsword and
  named armor, eleven perks already in place (Colossus, Reach Advantage, Berserk, Killing
  Frenzy, Battle Forged, Sword Mastery, Underdog, Recover, Fortified Mind, Pathfinder, Brawny),
  and the **Nightblade** trait: +3 action points, +30 initiative, +12 melee skill, +10 melee
  defense, +20 hitpoints, +5 fatigue recovery, and the **Blink** skill.
- **Blink**: 3 AP, teleport to any free tile within 5. With 12 AP that is blink in, swing a
  greatsword, blink out. Under Autopilot Expert 1.2.0+ he walks and blinks in the same turn to
  reach a target seven tiles away and still swing, goes for archers and wounded enemies first,
  blinks out again once he has swung and the mob is closing, and when nothing is in reach he
  walks and blinks to close the distance instead of just walking.
- **Nine companions** with clear roles and matching kit, talents and a war dog each: two
  shield-bearers, two two-handers, two polearms, two archers, one bannerman. All level 4 with
  their attribute and perk points left for Level-Up Pilot to spend on day one.
- 3000 crowns, supplies, medicine, ammo and repair parts.

## Wolfeo's Hunt (difficulty: hard)

Same avatar, four hunters instead of a line: two archers, a beast hunter with spear and shield, a
houndmaster with two dogs. 1200 crowns.

## The Sworn Five (difficulty: medium)

Five friends whose builds lean on each other. Everyone carries the **Sworn** trait: +2 action
points, +10 initiative, +10 resolve, +3 fatigue recovery.

- **Sable the Knife**, your avatar. Assassin, level 5, named qatal dagger, black leather armor,
  and nine perks in place (Dagger Mastery, Backstabber, Dodge, Nimble, Duelist, Overwhelm,
  Footwork, Relentless, Killing Frenzy). The **Knife in the Dark** trait adds one more action
  point, +25 initiative, +10 melee skill, +10 melee and ranged defense, +15 hitpoints.
- **Wren and Rook**, twin hunters. War bow and quiver each, light lamellar, Bow Mastery for both,
  Bullseye on one and Overwhelm on the other.
- **Halvard Longarm**, sellsword with a named spear, heater shield and medium mail. Spear Mastery
  and Fast Adaption.
- **Bram the Wall**, retired soldier in a coat of scales with sword and kite shield. Taunt, Shield
  Expert and Rally the Troops: he draws the blows and steadies the others.
- 2000 crowns, full supplies, spare quivers, and two treasures to sell.

Without the Blazing Deserts content Sable is a killer-on-the-run with a named dagger instead.

## Notes

- Rename Wolfeo in the character screen if you like; nothing depends on the name.
- Both starts spawn on a road next to a village, like the stock origins.
- Requires Modern Hooks. Works best with Autopilot Expert 1.2.0+ (Blink AI) and Level-Up Pilot.
